Illustrative Mathematics K–5 · Grade 3

Grade 3

Pick a unit. Learn it step by step, then practice it with a game. Each one lines up with a unit from class.

  • Unit 1

    Introducing Multiplication

    Read picture and bar graphs where each picture counts for 2, 5, or 10, then multiply with equal groups and arrays, find unknown numbers, and turn factors around.

    Learn →Play Orchard Market →

  • Unit 2

    Area & Multiplication

    Cover shapes with square units, multiply side lengths to find the area of a rectangle, pick square centimeters, inches, feet, or meters, and find the area of figures made of rectangles.

    Learn →Play Tile Town →

  • Unit 3

    Wrapping Up Addition & Subtraction within 1,000

    Add and subtract within 1,000 on open number lines, by place, and with the standard algorithms, round to the nearest ten and hundred, estimate to check, and solve two-step stories.

    Learn →Play Treasure Cove →

  • Unit 4

    Relating Multiplication to Division

    Split things into equal groups, find a missing factor, spot patterns in the multiplication table, break apart rectangles, and multiply and divide larger numbers with base-ten blocks and area diagrams.

    Learn →Play Toy Workshop →

  • Unit 5

    Fractions as Numbers

    Cut shapes into equal parts, build fractions from unit fractions, find fractions on the number line, write whole numbers as fractions, find equivalent fractions, and compare fractions.

    Learn →Play Fraction Farm →

  • Unit 6

    Measuring Length, Time, Liquid Volume & Weight

    Measure to the half and fourth inch, read and make line plots, weigh in grams and kilograms, measure liters, tell time to the minute, find elapsed time, and solve measurement stories.

    Learn →Play Science Fair →
