## Curriculum reference: IM Grade 6

The current version is **IM v.360** (accessim.org), which replaced v.III. The lesson titles below come from the v.III teacher guide (im.kendallhunt.com/MS/teachers/1/<unit>/index.html), because AccessIM doesn't show titles without a sign-in. v.360 uses the same unit order and titles; lesson counts and section groupings can differ (noted where known). Check against the teacher's materials before relying on exact lesson numbers.

### Unit 1: Area and Surface Area
Find areas of polygons by decomposing and rearranging, use base and height for parallelograms and triangles, and use nets to find surface area.
- **Reasoning to Find Area:** 1 Tiling the Plane · 2 Finding Area by Decomposing and Rearranging · 3 Reasoning to Find Area
- **Parallelograms:** 4 Parallelograms · 5 Bases and Heights of Parallelograms · 6 Area of Parallelograms
- **Triangles:** 7 From Parallelograms to Triangles · 8 Area of Triangles · 9 Formula for the Area of a Triangle · 10 Bases and Heights of Triangles
- **Polygons:** 11 Polygons
- **Surface Area:** 12 What is Surface Area? · 13 Polyhedra · 14 Nets and Surface Area · 15 More Nets, More Surface Area · 16 Distinguishing Between Surface Area and Volume
- **Squares and Cubes:** 17 Squares and Cubes · 18 Surface Area of a Cube
- **Let's Put it to Work:** 19 Designing a Tent

### Unit 2: Introducing Ratios
Ratio language, equivalent ratios, and representing them with discrete diagrams, double number lines, tables, and tape diagrams.
- **What are Ratios?:** 1 Introducing Ratios and Ratio Language · 2 Representing Ratios with Diagrams
- **Equivalent Ratios:** 3 Recipes · 4 Color Mixtures · 5 Defining Equivalent Ratios
- **Representing Equivalent Ratios:** 6 Introducing Double Number Line Diagrams · 7 Creating Double Number Line Diagrams · 8 How Much for One? · 9 Constant Speed · 10 Comparing Situations by Examining Ratios
- **Solving Ratio and Rate Problems:** 11 Representing Ratios with Tables · 12 Navigating a Table of Equivalent Ratios · 13 Tables and Double Number Line Diagrams · 14 Solving Equivalent Ratio Problems
- **Part-part-whole Ratios:** 15 Part-Part-Whole Ratios · 16 Solving More Ratio Problems
- **Let's Put it to Work:** 17 A Fermi Problem

(v.360 groups these the same way, as sections A–F.)

### Unit 3: Unit Rates and Percentages
Unit rates, speed and pace, unit price, measurement conversion, and percentages (percent of a quantity, finding the whole, finding the percentage) using double number lines, tables, and tape diagrams.
- **Units of Measurement:** 1 The Burj Khalifa
- **Unit Conversion:** 2 Anchoring Units of Measurement · 3 Measuring with Different-Sized Units · 4 Converting Units
- **Rates:** 5 Comparing Speeds and Prices · 6 Interpreting Rates · 7 Equivalent Ratios Have the Same Unit Rates · 8 More about Constant Speed · 9 Solving Rate Problems
- **Percentages:** 10 What Are Percentages? · 11 Percentages and Double Number Lines · 12 Percentages and Tape Diagrams · 13 Benchmark Percentages · 14 Solving Percentage Problems · 15 Finding This Percent of That · 16 Finding the Percentage
- **Let's Put it to Work:** 17 Painting a Room

(v.360 groups these as A: 1–3, B: 4–9, C: 10–16, D: 17.)

### Unit 4: Dividing Fractions
Meanings of division, "how many groups?" and "how much in each group?", dividing by multiplying by the reciprocal, and fractional side lengths in area and volume.
- **Making Sense of Division:** 1 Size of Divisor and Size of Quotient · 2 Meanings of Division · 3 Interpreting Division Situations
- **Meanings of Fraction Division:** 4 How Many Groups? (Part 1) · 5 How Many Groups? (Part 2) · 6 Using Diagrams to Find the Number of Groups · 7 What Fraction of a Group? · 8 How Much in Each Group? (Part 1) · 9 How Much in Each Group? (Part 2)
- **Algorithm for Fraction Division:** 10 Dividing by Unit and Non-Unit Fractions · 11 Using an Algorithm to Divide Fractions
- **Fractions in Lengths, Areas, and Volumes:** 12 Fractional Lengths · 13 Rectangles with Fractional Side Lengths · 14 Fractional Lengths in Triangles and Prisms · 15 Volume of Prisms
- **Let's Put It to Work:** 16 Solving Problems Involving Fractions · 17 Fitting Boxes into Boxes

### Unit 5: Arithmetic in Base Ten
Add, subtract, multiply, and divide multi-digit whole numbers and decimals with efficient algorithms, and apply them to real-world problems.
- **Warming Up to Decimals:** 1 Using Decimals in a Shopping Context
- **Adding and Subtracting Decimals:** 2 Using Diagrams to Represent Addition and Subtraction · 3 Adding and Subtracting Decimals with Few Non-Zero Digits · 4 Adding and Subtracting Decimals with Many Non-Zero Digits
- **Multiplying Decimals:** 5 Decimal Points in Products · 6 Methods for Multiplying Decimals · 7 Using Diagrams to Represent Multiplication · 8 Calculating Products of Decimals
- **Dividing Decimals:** 9 Using the Partial Quotients Method · 10 Using Long Division · 11 Dividing Numbers that Result in Decimals · 12 Dividing Decimals by Whole Numbers · 13 Dividing Decimals by Decimals
- **Let's Put It to Work:** 14 Using Operations on Decimals to Solve Problems · 15 Making and Measuring Boxes

### Unit 6: Expressions and Equations
Variables, coefficients, and solutions; solving one-step equations; equivalent expressions and the distributive property; exponents; and relationships between two quantities in tables, graphs, and equations.
- **Equations in One Variable:** 1 Tape Diagrams and Equations · 2 Truth and Equations · 3 Staying in Balance · 4 Practice Solving Equations and Representing Situations with Equations · 5 A New Way to Interpret a over b
- **Equal and Equivalent:** 6 Write Expressions Where Letters Stand for Numbers · 7 Revisit Percentages · 8 Equal and Equivalent · 9 The Distributive Property, Part 1 · 10 The Distributive Property, Part 2 · 11 The Distributive Property, Part 3
- **Expressions with Exponents:** 12 Meaning of Exponents · 13 Expressions with Exponents · 14 Evaluating Expressions with Exponents · 15 Equivalent Exponential Expressions
- **Relationships Between Quantities:** 16 Two Related Quantities, Part 1 · 17 Two Related Quantities, Part 2 · 18 More Relationships
- **Let's Put it to Work:** 19 Tables, Equations, and Graphs, Oh My!

### Unit 7: Rational Numbers
Signed numbers in context, absolute value, inequalities, the four-quadrant coordinate plane, and common factors and multiples.
- **Negative Numbers and Absolute Value:** 1 Positive and Negative Numbers · 2 Points on the Number Line · 3 Comparing Positive and Negative Numbers · 4 Ordering Rational Numbers · 5 Using Negative Numbers to Make Sense of Contexts · 6 Absolute Value of Numbers · 7 Comparing Numbers and Distance from Zero
- **Inequalities:** 8 Writing and Graphing Inequalities · 9 Solutions of Inequalities · 10 Interpreting Inequalities
- **The Coordinate Plane:** 11 Points on the Coordinate Plane · 12 Constructing the Coordinate Plane · 13 Interpreting Points on a Coordinate Plane · 14 Distances on a Coordinate Plane · 15 Shapes on the Coordinate Plane
- **Common Factors and Common Multiples:** 16 Common Factors · 17 Common Multiples · 18 Using Common Multiples and Common Factors
- **Let's Put it to Work:** 19 Drawing on the Coordinate Plane

### Unit 8: Data Sets and Distributions
Statistical questions, dot plots, histograms, and box plots; mean and MAD, median and IQR; describing and comparing distributions.
- **Data, Variability, and Statistical Questions:** 1 Got Data? · 2 Statistical Questions
- **Dot Plots and Histograms:** 3 Representing Data Graphically · 4 Dot Plots · 5 Using Dot Plots to Answer Statistical Questions · 6 Interpreting Histograms · 7 Using Histograms to Answer Statistical Questions · 8 Describing Distributions on Histograms
- **Measures of Center and Variability:** 9 Mean · 10 Finding and Interpreting the Mean as the Balance Point · 11 Variability and MAD · 12 Using Mean and MAD to Make Comparisons
- **Median and IQR:** 13 Median · 14 Comparing Mean and Median · 15 Quartiles and Interquartile Range · 16 Box Plots · 17 Using Box Plots
- **Let's Put it to Work:** 18 Using Data to Solve Problems

### Unit 9: Putting It All Together (optional)
Applies measurement, conversions, surface area and volume, ratios, rates, and percentages to open-ended problems: Fermi estimates, population, geometry puzzles, and voting.
- **Making Connections:** 1 Fermi Problems · 2 If Our Class Were the World · 3 Rectangle Madness
- **Voting:** 4 How Do We Choose? · 5 More than Two Choices · 6 Picking Representatives
