How a round works

Each station deals 8 fresh problems (the Final Lap deals 10). A right answer on the first try earns 10 points. After a wrong answer, the game names the likely mistake and gives one more try for 5 points.

The "Show me" hint

Hints use the tools from class: double number lines, a "1" row in a table, benchmark fractions like 25% = 1/4, and the value of every box in a percent tape. A used hint caps that problem at 5 points.

Mistakes it catches

Multiplying when a conversion needs dividing, using the flipped unit rate, thinking a bigger pace is faster, reading 25% as “divide by 25,” and mixing up the part and the whole.

Not covered

Lesson 1 (an estimation launch) and Lesson 17 (Painting a Room, a multi-step project) are open-ended, so they work better in class than as scored problems.
