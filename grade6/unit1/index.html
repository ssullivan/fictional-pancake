How a round works

Each zone deals 8 fresh problems (the Final Blueprint deals 10). A right answer on the first try earns 10 points. After a wrong answer, the game names the likely mistake and gives one more try for 5 points.

The "Show me" hint

Hints use the same moves as class: cut and slide a parallelogram into a rectangle, double a triangle into a parallelogram, split a shape into rectangles. A used hint caps that problem at 5 points.

Frame & Subtract

Shapes drawn inside a rectangle, solved like the class worksheets: rectangle area minus the unshaded pieces. A wrong first answer switches to Build it, which checks each piece one at a time. Dented shapes unlock after 2 stars in that zone.

Mistakes it catches

Using the slanted side instead of the height, forgetting to halve a triangle, counting only 3 faces of a prism, and mixing up surface area with volume.
