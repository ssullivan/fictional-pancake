Angles & Angle Measurement

Short lessons you can play with. Tell lines, rays, and segments apart, turn lines to make them parallel or perpendicular, measure and draw angles on a protractor, and find unknown angles, then check yourself.

How it works

Six short chapters follow the unit’s lessons in order. Each step shows one idea with something to tap or move, then a quick check. A wrong answer names the likely mistake and gives one more try. Progress is saved on this device.

Numbers

Angles are in whole degrees, in multiples of 5 when read from a protractor. A right angle is 90°, a straight angle 180°, and a full turn 360°. The protractor’s inner scale counts from 0 on the right and its outer scale from 0 on the left, as on most classroom protractors.

Words to know

Ray: part of a line with one endpoint. Segment: part of a line with two endpoints. Parallel: lines that never meet. Perpendicular: lines that meet at a right angle. Acute, right, obtuse, straight: angles less than 90°, exactly 90°, between 90° and 180°, and exactly 180°.
