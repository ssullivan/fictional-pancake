For grown-ups

How a round works

Each station deals 8 fresh problems (Stargazing deals 10). A right answer on the first try earns 10 points. After a wrong answer, the game names the likely mistake and gives one more try for 5 points. At Stargazing, each right answer lights one of 10 stars.

The "Show me" hint

Hints use the pictures from class: fraction strips in equal groups, jumps on a number line, parts crossed out to subtract, line plots, and hundred grids for tenths and hundredths. A used hint caps that problem at 5 points.

Mistakes it catches

Multiplying the denominator along with the numerator, adding the number of groups to the fraction, adding the denominators, adding when the story takes away, taking the smaller fraction from the larger instead of trading a whole, counting the Xs at a value as more than it, and adding tenths and hundredths as if they were the same size.

Numbers

Denominators are 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12, and 100, as in the unit. Typed fraction answers can be written like 7/4 or 1 3/4. Equal groups stay within 4 wholes, and mixed numbers within 6.
