From Hundredths to Hundred-thousands

Short lessons you can play with. Write tenths and hundredths as decimals, build numbers up to 1,000,000, compare and round them, and add and subtract with the standard algorithm, then check yourself.

For grown-ups

How it works

Seven short chapters follow the unit’s lessons in order. Each step shows one idea with something to tap or move, then a quick check. A wrong answer names the likely mistake and gives one more try. Progress is saved on this device.

Numbers

Decimals go to hundredths and come with hundred grids and money or measurement stories. Whole numbers go up to 1,000,000 with only a few nonzero digits. Each problem for adding or subtracting in columns needs at most one regroup, except the ones about zeros, where regrouping across the zeros is the point.

Words to know

Decimal: a way to write tenths and hundredths, like 0.47 for 47/100. Place value: what a digit is worth because of its place: the 3 in 305,020 is worth 300,000. Round: find the nearest multiple of 1,000, 10,000, or 100,000. Standard algorithm: adding or subtracting one place at a time in columns. Regroup: trade 10 of one unit for 1 of the next (or back).
