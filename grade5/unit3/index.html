For grown-ups

How a round works

Each station deals 8 fresh problems (the Harvest Fair deals 10). A right answer on the first try earns 10 points. After a wrong answer, the game names the likely mistake and gives one more try for 5 points. At the Harvest Fair, each right answer adds one of 10 vegetables to the prize basket.

The "Show me" hint

Hints use the pictures from class: a unit square cut into columns and rows, with a fraction of a fraction shaded; a fraction strip cut into smaller equal parts; and wholes cut into unit fractions to count. A used hint caps that problem at 5 points.

Mistakes it catches

Adding the denominators (or the numerators and denominators) instead of multiplying, adding two fractions instead of taking one of the other, taking all of a fraction instead of part of it, multiplying when a story shares, dividing the wrong way around, and thinking that multiplying always makes a number bigger.

Numbers

The fractions multiplied and divided have denominators from 2 to 6, so products have denominators up to 36. Dividing uses only a unit fraction ÷ a whole number up to 5, or a whole number up to 6 ÷ a unit fraction, as in the unit. Answers can be typed as fractions (3/10).
