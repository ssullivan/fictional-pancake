Multiply & Divide Fractions

Short lessons you can play with. Take a piece of a part on a square, multiply any two fractions, share a unit fraction, count how many unit fractions fit, and pick the operation a story needs, then check yourself.

For grown-ups

How it works

Five short chapters follow the unit’s lessons in order. Each step shows one idea with something to tap or move, then a quick check. A wrong answer names the likely mistake and gives one more try. Progress is saved on this device.

Numbers

Fractions multiplied or divided have denominators from 2 to 6. Dividing uses only a unit fraction ÷ a whole number, or a whole number ÷ a unit fraction, as in the unit. Answers can be typed as fractions (3/10).

Words to know

Unit fraction: a fraction with 1 on top, like 1/4. Product: the answer to a multiplication. Quotient: the answer to a division.

Not covered
