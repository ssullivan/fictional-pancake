Illustrative Mathematics K–5 · Grade 5

Grade 5

Pick a unit. Learn it step by step, then practice it with a game. Each one lines up with a unit from class.

  • Unit 1

    Finding Volume

    Build figures from unit cubes, pack boxes layer by layer, multiply length × width × height, and find the volume of figures made of two boxes.

    Learn →Play Cargo Port →

  • Unit 2

    Fractions as Quotients & Fraction Multiplication

    Share sandwiches to see that a ÷ b is a/b, take a fraction of a whole number, and find areas of rectangles with fraction sides.

    Learn →Play Picnic Park →

  • Unit 3

    Multiplying & Dividing Fractions

    Take a fraction of a fraction on a square, multiply tops and bottoms, share a unit fraction, and count how many unit fractions fit in a whole number.

    Learn →Play Community Garden →

  • Unit 4

    Multiplying & Dividing Multi-digit Numbers

    Estimate products, multiply with partial products and the standard algorithm, divide by two-digit numbers with partial quotients, and solve big problems.

    Learn →Play Record Breakers →

  • Unit 5

    Place Value Patterns & Decimal Operations

    Zoom in to thousandths, compare and round decimals, and add, subtract, multiply, and divide with tenths and hundredths.

    Learn →Play Track Meet →

  • Unit 6

    More Decimal & Fraction Operations

    Move digits with powers of 10, convert metric and customary units, add and subtract fractions with unlike denominators, read line plots, and predict the size of products.

    Learn →Play Trail Rangers →

  • Unit 7

    Shapes on the Coordinate Plane

    Plot points on the coordinate grid, sort quadrilaterals and triangles, follow two number patterns at once, and measure rectangles from their corners.

    Learn →Play Map Makers →
