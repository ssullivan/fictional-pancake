Track Meet

Welcome to the track meet! Read times to the thousandth of a second, judge photo finishes, round for the scoreboard, add relay legs, measure long jumps, and fill cups at the water station. Then run the victory lap. Each station matches lessons from class.

For grown-ups

How a round works

Each station deals 8 fresh problems (the Victory Lap deals 10). A right answer on the first try earns 10 points. After a wrong answer, the game names the likely mistake and gives one more try for 5 points. On the Victory Lap, each right answer lights one of 10 flags around the track.

The "Show me" hint

Hints use the pictures from class: hundred grids with a hundredth blown up into thousandths, place-value charts, number lines for rounding, tenths strips in equal groups, and squares cut into hundredths for multiplying. A used hint caps that problem at 5 points.

Mistakes it catches

Leaving out a 0 that holds a place, reading a digit’s place wrong, thinking more digits means a bigger number, rounding the wrong way or to the wrong place, lining up the last digits instead of the decimal points, and products and quotients with the decimal point in the wrong place (tenths × tenths make hundredths).

Numbers

Decimals are read, compared, and rounded to thousandths. Adding, subtracting, multiplying, and dividing stay in tenths and hundredths, with at most one regroup, and each product or quotient comes from a basic fact. Typed answers must be exact to the thousandth.
