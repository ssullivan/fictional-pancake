Decimals to Thousandths

Short lessons you can play with. Zoom in to thousandths, compare and round decimals, line up the decimal points to add and subtract, and multiply and divide with tenths, then check yourself.

For grown-ups

How it works

Six short chapters follow the unit’s lessons in order. Each step shows one idea with something to tap or move, then a quick check. A wrong answer names the likely mistake and gives one more try. Progress is saved on this device.

Numbers

Decimals are read, compared, and rounded to thousandths. Adding and subtracting stay in tenths and hundredths; multiplying and dividing use tenths and hundredths with a basic fact at the core, as in the unit.

Words to know

Thousandth: one of 1,000 equal parts of a whole, 0.001. Expanded form: a number written as the sum of its places, like 3 + 0.4 + 0.07. Round: find the nearest number with fewer places.
