Putting It All Together

Short lessons to look back on the year. Make the largest product and divide in chunks, fill and estimate giant wagons, add and subtract fractions, and work with decimals and fraction products, then check yourself.

For grown-ups

How it works

Four short chapters review the year in the unit’s lesson order. Each step shows one idea with something to tap or move, then a quick check. A wrong answer names the likely mistake and gives one more try. Progress is saved on this device.

Numbers

The same limits as the rest of the year: products up to 3 digits × 2 digits, friendly two-digit divisors, whole-number edges, common denominators of 24 or less, and decimals in tenths and hundredths.

Words to know

Estimate: an answer close to the exact one, found with friendly numbers. Common denominator: a denominator both fractions can be written with. Product: the answer to a multiplication.

Not covered

Problem Solving with Volume: Water (Lesson 9) is optional, and the Creation and Design lessons (Lessons 14–18) are open-ended. Practice it all in Moving Up Day, the game for this unit.
