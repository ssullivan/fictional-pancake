Moving Up Day

It’s the last day of fifth grade! Run the number booth, pack cupcakes, fill the big wagon, work the fraction fair, race in the decimal derby, and hand out ribbons. Then graduate to Grade 6. Each station matches lessons from class.

For grown-ups

How a round works

Each station deals 8 fresh problems (Graduation deals 10). A right answer on the first try earns 10 points. After a wrong answer, the game names the likely mistake and gives one more try for 5 points. At Graduation, each right answer sends one of 10 caps into the air.

The "Show me" hint

Hints use the pictures from the year: the standard algorithm row by row, partial quotients, boxes of cubes, fraction strips cut to a common denominator, and squares cut into parts for multiplying. A used hint caps that problem at 5 points.

Mistakes it catches

Leaving out the 0 in the tens row, stopping after one partial quotient, adding edges instead of multiplying, filling in the empty corner of a two-box figure, estimates 10 times too big, adding the tops and bottoms of fractions, lining up the last digits of decimals, and putting the decimal point in the wrong place.

Numbers

The same limits as the rest of the year: products up to 3 digits × 2 digits, friendly two-digit divisors, whole-number edges, common denominators of 24 or less, and decimals in tenths and hundredths. Answers can be typed as fractions or mixed numbers.

Learn it first

Each station matches a chapter of the Learn page for this unit, which walks through the ideas step by step.

Not covered

Problem Solving with Volume: Water (Lesson 9) is optional, and the Creation and Design lessons (Lessons 14–18) are open-ended, so there are no stations for them.
