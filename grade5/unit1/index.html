Cargo Port

Welcome to the port! Count cubes in the yard, pack boxes layer by layer, measure crates, stack odd-shaped cargo, and solve the harbor’s puzzles. Then load the cargo ship. Each station matches lessons from class.

For grown-ups

How a round works

Each station deals 8 fresh problems (Full Load deals 10). A right answer on the first try earns 10 points. After a wrong answer, the game names the likely mistake and gives one more try for 5 points. In Full Load, each right answer loads one of 10 containers onto the ship.

The "Show me" hint

Hints use the pictures from class: the back row of a figure in another color, a box split into layers of cubes, and the base and height of a box. A used hint caps that problem at 5 points.

Mistakes it catches

Counting only the cubes you can see, counting one layer or one row, adding the edges instead of multiplying, stopping at the area of the base, filling in the empty corner of a figure made of two boxes, multiplying the volumes of two parts, subtracting the base or dividing by one edge to find a missing edge, and measuring volume in square units.

Numbers

Edges are whole numbers up to 10 in centimeters, inches, feet, or meters, and volumes stay at 400 cubic units or less, so the multiplying can be done in your head.

Learn it first

Each station matches a chapter of the Learn page for this unit, which walks through the ideas step by step.
