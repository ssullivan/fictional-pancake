Shapes on the Grid

Short lessons you can play with. Plot points on the coordinate grid, sort quadrilaterals and triangles by their sides and angles, follow two number patterns at once, and measure rectangles from their corners, then check yourself.

For grown-ups

How it works

Five short chapters follow the unit’s lessons in order. Each step shows one idea with something to tap or move, then a quick check. A wrong answer names the likely mistake and gives one more try. Progress is saved on this device.

Numbers

The grid is the first quadrant, with whole-number coordinates up to 10 across and 8 up. A trapezoid is any quadrilateral with at least one pair of parallel sides, as in the unit.

Words to know

Coordinates: the pair of numbers that says where a point is, across first. Parallel: running the same way, never meeting. Isosceles: at least two equal sides. Scalene: no equal sides. Ordered pair: two numbers written in order, like (2, 6).

Not covered

Points on the Coordinate Grid (Lesson 2), Trapezoids (Lesson 5), and Patterns and Ordered Pairs (Lesson 11) are optional lessons. Practice it all in Map Makers, the game for this unit.
