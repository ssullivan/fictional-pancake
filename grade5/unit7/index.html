Map Makers

Welcome, map makers! Find spots on the grid at base camp, survey the shapes of fields, sort the triangle peaks, follow two trail patterns at once, and measure land plots. Then go on the treasure hunt. Each station matches lessons from class.

For grown-ups

How a round works

Each station deals 8 fresh problems (the Treasure Hunt deals 10). A right answer on the first try earns 10 points. After a wrong answer, the game names the likely mistake and gives one more try for 5 points. On the Treasure Hunt, each right answer digs up one of 10 gold coins.

The "Show me" hint

Hints point back to the grid: count across first, then up; check each corner against a square corner of the grid; and look for opposite sides that run the same way. A used hint caps that problem at 5 points.

Mistakes it catches

Swapping the two coordinates, adding instead of subtracting to find a distance, giving a true name that isn’t the most specific one, thinking every rectangle is a square or every trapezoid a parallelogram, mixing up isosceles and scalene or acute and obtuse, counting the start of a pattern as a step, and mixing up perimeter and area.

Numbers

The grid is the first quadrant, 10 across and 8 up, with whole-number coordinates. A trapezoid is any quadrilateral with at least one pair of parallel sides, as in the unit, so every parallelogram is a trapezoid. Patterns start at 0 and add up to 16.

Learn it first

Each station matches a chapter of the Learn page for this unit, which walks through the ideas step by step.
