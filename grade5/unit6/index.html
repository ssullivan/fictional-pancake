Trail Rangers

Welcome, rangers! Work the ranger station’s powers of 10, read the trail markers in metric units, measure the rope bridge, mix up trail mix, read the rain gauge, and check the view from the lookout. Then climb to the summit. Each station matches lessons from class.

For grown-ups

How a round works

Each station deals 8 fresh problems (the Summit deals 10). A right answer on the first try earns 10 points. After a wrong answer, the game names the likely mistake and gives one more try for 5 points. On the way to the Summit, each right answer climbs one of 10 switchbacks.

The "Show me" hint

Hints use the pictures from class: place-value charts with the digits moved, fraction strips cut to a common denominator, line plots with the spread or a column marked, and tapes that compare a number with a product. A used hint caps that problem at 5 points.

Mistakes it catches

Reading 104 as 10 × 4, moving the digits the wrong way or too few places, dividing when converting to a smaller unit, adding meters to kilometers, writing feet and inches side by side, adding the tops and the bottoms of fractions, changing a denominator but not its numerator, subtracting a mixed number without trading a whole, counting the amounts on a line plot instead of the days, and thinking multiplying always makes a number bigger.

Numbers

Powers of 10 go up to 106. Metric conversions use km, m, cm, mm, L, mL, kg, and g; customary use yards, feet, and inches. Fractions have denominators 2 to 12 with a common denominator of 24 or less. Answers can be typed as fractions (5/6) or mixed numbers (1 3/4).
