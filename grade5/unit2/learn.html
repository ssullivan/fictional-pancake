Fractions & Sharing

Short lessons you can play with. Share sandwiches fairly, see why a ÷ b is a fraction, take a fraction of a number, and find areas of rectangles with fraction sides, then check yourself.

Steps done 0/0

For grown-ups

How it works

Five short chapters follow the unit’s lessons in order. Each step shows one idea with something to tap or move, then a quick check. A wrong answer names the likely mistake and gives one more try. Progress is saved on this device.

Numbers

Denominators are 2, 3, 4, 5, and 6. Sharing goes up to 12 things, fractions of a number split 12 or a few pounds, and rectangles are at most 5 units on a side. Answers can be typed as fractions (7/4) or mixed numbers (1 3/4).

Words to know

Quotient: the answer to a division; a ÷ b = a/b. Mixed number: a whole number and a fraction, like 1 3/4. Unit square: a square 1 unit on each side, with an area of 1 square unit.

Not covered
