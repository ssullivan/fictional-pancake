Picnic Park

Welcome to the picnic! Share sandwiches fairly, pour lemonade into pitchers, pick berries, and lay out blankets and garden beds. Then fly the kites. Each station matches lessons from class.

For grown-ups

How a round works

Each station deals 8 fresh problems (the Kite Festival deals 10). A right answer on the first try earns 10 points. After a wrong answer, the game names the likely mistake and gives one more try for 5 points. At the Kite Festival, each right answer sends one of 10 kites into the sky.

The "Show me" hint

Hints use the pictures from class: sandwiches cut into a piece for each person, fraction strips, tape diagrams split into equal parts, and rectangles on a grid of unit squares, split into a whole-number part and a fraction part. A used hint caps that problem at 5 points.

Mistakes it catches

Dividing the wrong way around (b ÷ a), giving one piece of one sandwich instead of a piece of each, stopping at a remainder or at the whole-number part, multiplying instead of dividing to find a fraction of a number, taking one part instead of several, adding the side lengths instead of multiplying, and multiplying only the whole-number part of a mixed number.

Numbers

Denominators are 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, and 8. Shares come from at most 12 things, fractions of a number divide evenly or stay under 5 wholes, and rectangles are at most 5 units on a side. Answers can be typed as fractions (7/4) or mixed numbers (1 3/4).
