Record Breakers

Welcome to the record hall! Guess big products, build them with partial products, stack them with the standard algorithm, line up dancers, fill bowls of noodle soup, and run the recycling center. Then go for the world record. Each station matches lessons from class.

For grown-ups

How a round works

Each station deals 8 fresh problems (World Record deals 10). A right answer on the first try earns 10 points. After a wrong answer, the game names the likely mistake and gives one more try for 5 points. In World Record, each right answer earns one of 10 medals.

The "Show me" hint

Hints use the pictures from class: area diagrams split by place value, the standard algorithm worked row by row, partial quotients taken away in chunks, and rectangles with a missing side. A used hint caps that problem at 5 points.

Mistakes it catches

Estimates with a zero too many or too few, multiplying only some of the parts, leaving out the carries, leaving out the 0 in the tens row of the standard algorithm, stopping after the first partial quotient, subtracting or multiplying instead of dividing, stopping after one step of a two-step problem, and leaving paper behind instead of making one more trip.

Numbers

Multiplying goes up to 4 digits × 1 digit and 3 digits × 2 digits, with few nonzero digits and at most two carries. Dividing goes up to 2,500 by friendly two-digit numbers (11, 12, 15, 20, 25, 30, 40, 50), with no remainder unless the story says what to do with it.

Learn it first

Each station matches a chapter of the Learn page for this unit, which walks through the ideas step by step.
