Illustrative Mathematics · Grade 7

Grade 7

Pick a unit. Learn it step by step, then practice it with a game. Each one lines up with a unit from class.

  • Unit 1

    Scale Drawings Model Makers

    Spot scaled copies, find scale factors, watch area grow by the factor times itself, read maps and floor plans, change scales, and build models at 1 to 50.

    Learn →Play →

  • Unit 2

    Introducing Proportional Relationships Food Truck Rally

    Find the constant of proportionality in tables, write and use equations like y = kx, test whether a relationship is proportional, and read graphs through (0, 0).

    Learn →Play →

  • Unit 3

    Measuring Circles Wheel Works

    Find the radius and diameter, discover π, roll wheels, cut circles into wedges to find their area, and decide when a job needs the circumference or the area.

    Learn →Play →

  • Unit 4

    Proportional Relationships & Percentages Mall Rush

    Find rates from fractions, grow things by half again, raise and lower prices by a percent, add tax and tips, hunt for deals, and measure percent error.

    Learn →Play →

  • Unit 5

    Rational Number Arithmetic Deep Dive

    Add and subtract signed numbers with temperatures, depths, and jumps on a number line, multiply and divide them, use negative rates, and solve equations with negative numbers.

    Learn →Play →
