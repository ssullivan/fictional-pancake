For grown-ups

How a round works

Each station deals 8 fresh problems (the Rally Finals deal 10). A right answer on the first try earns 10 points. After a wrong answer, the game names the likely mistake and gives one more try for 5 points. A question with only two choices (proportional or not?) gets one try.

The "Show me" hint

Hints write the “× k” beside each row of a table, mark the point (1, k) on a graph, and suggest dividing a y by its x to find the constant.

Numbers you can do in your head

Constants are friendly (2.50 dollars per taco, 50 miles per hour, 2/3 cup of sugar per cup of flour), tables use small whole numbers, and graphs stay in the first quadrant with gridlines that fit, so the challenge is the relationship, not the arithmetic.

Mistakes it catches

Dividing x by y instead of y by x, adding the same amount down a table instead of multiplying, an equation with the letters swapped, a line that misses (0, 0) taken for proportional, the numbers of a point swapped, and forgetting that swapping a graph’s axes turns the constant into its reciprocal.

Not covered
