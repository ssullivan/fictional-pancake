How a round works

Each station deals 8 fresh problems (the Model Expo deals 10). A right answer on the first try earns 10 points. After a wrong answer, the game names the likely mistake and gives one more try for 5 points. A question with only two choices (is it a scaled copy?) gets one try.

The "Show me" hint

Hints label the sides on the grid, mark the matching sides, cut a scaled-up shape into copies of the original to show why area grows by the scale factor times itself, and write the real length next to each road on a map.

Numbers you can do in your head

Shapes sit on a grid with whole-number sides, scale factors are whole numbers or halves (1/2, 3/2, 5/2), map lengths are whole or half centimeters, and scales use friendly numbers like 1 cm to 25 km or 1 to 50, so the challenge is the scaling, not the arithmetic.

Mistakes it catches

Adding the same amount to every side instead of multiplying, a stretched copy taken for a scaled one, the scale factor turned upside down, angles multiplied along with the sides, area multiplied by the scale factor only once, dividing by the scale when a real length needs multiplying, and a scale like 1 cm to 5 m read as 1 to 5.

Not covered

Lesson 13 (Draw It to Scale) is a project where students make their own floor plan, so it works better in class than as scored problems.
