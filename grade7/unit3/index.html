How a round works

Each station deals 8 fresh problems (the Grand Prix deals 10). A right answer on the first try earns 10 points. After a wrong answer, the game names the likely mistake and gives one more try for 5 points. A question with only two choices (circumference or area?) gets one try.

Answers with π

Most answers are typed in terms of π, the way IM writes them: for a circumference of 12π cm, type 12. Decimal answers with π ≈ 3.14 are multiple choice, so nobody has to multiply 3.14 by 37 in their head.

The "Show me" hint

Hints label the radius, diameter, and circumference on the circle, mark each turn of a rolling wheel, and cut a circle into wedges laid out like a parallelogram πr long and r tall, which is why the area is πr².

Mistakes it catches

Mixing up radius and diameter, πr for the circumference, squaring the diameter for the area, 2πr and πr² swapped, one turn of a wheel for many, the whole circle for half of it, the curved edge without the straight sides, and doubling the area when a pizza is twice as wide.

Not covered

Lessons 1 and 6 (measuring real circles and estimating areas on a grid) are hands-on, and Lesson 11 (Stained-Glass Windows) is a design project, so they work better in class than as scored problems.
