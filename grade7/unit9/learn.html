For grown-ups

How it works

Four chapters follow the unit’s lessons in order. Each step shows one idea with something to change, then a quick check. A wrong answer names the likely mistake and gives one more try.

An optional unit that uses the whole year

IM’s Unit 9 has no new standards: it puts the year’s math to work in running a restaurant and planning a race. Scaling recipes uses proportional relationships (Units 2 and 4), markups use percents (Unit 4), the floor plan is a scale drawing (Unit 1), the trundle wheel uses circumference (Unit 3), and percent error comes from Unit 4.

Practice

Each chapter ends with a link to the matching station in Grand Opening, the practice game for this unit. Progress is saved on this device.

Not covered

Lesson 6 (Fermi problems), Lesson 11 (building a trundle wheel), and Lesson 13 (designing a 5K course) are open-ended or hands-on, so they work better in class.
