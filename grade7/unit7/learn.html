Angles, Triangles & Prisms

Short lessons you can play with. Find complementary, supplementary, and vertical angles, write equations for angles, test which sides make a triangle, slice solids, and find the volume and surface area of prisms, then check yourself. When you’re ready, practice in Skate Park Builders.

For grown-ups

How it works

Five chapters follow the unit’s lessons in order. Each step shows one idea with something to change, then a quick check. A wrong answer names the likely mistake and gives one more try.

Angles as equations

Angle relationships turn into equations: complementary angles add to 90°, supplementary to 180°, and vertical angles are equal. The unit uses that to practice the equation solving from Unit 6.

Volume and surface area

Volume is the base’s area times the height, for any right prism, so finding the area of odd-shaped bases (by splitting them up or taking a piece away) matters. Surface area adds every face. Students often mix the two up, so the last step sorts jobs by which one they need.
