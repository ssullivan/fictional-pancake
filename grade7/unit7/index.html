How a round works

Each station deals 8 fresh problems (the Skate Jam deals 10). A right answer on the first try earns 10 points. After a wrong answer, the game names the likely mistake and gives one more try for 5 points. A question with only two choices (surface area or volume?) gets one try.

The "Show me" hint

Hints name the angle relationship (complementary, supplementary, vertical), add the two shortest sides of a triangle, draw the cut face beside a sliced solid, and split a base into rectangles and triangles.

Numbers you can do in your head

Angles are multiples of 5, equations have whole-number solutions, sides are at most 15, and prisms have whole-number edges, so the challenge is the geometry, not the arithmetic.

Mistakes it catches

Using 180° for a right angle (or 90° for a straight line), taking vertical angles for supplementary ones, solving for x when the angle is asked (and the other way round), sides that can’t reach, a triangle’s area without the half, forgetting a cut-out corner, counting only three faces of a box, and volume when the job needs surface area.

Not covered

Lessons 6 and 7 (building polygons from straws and paper) and Lesson 17 (building prisms) are hands-on, so they work better in class than as scored problems.
