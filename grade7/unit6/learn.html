Expressions, Equations & Inequalities

Short lessons you can play with. Match tape diagrams to equations, solve on a balanced hanger and with negative numbers, graph and solve inequalities, and write equivalent expressions, then check yourself. When you’re ready, practice in Code Breakers.

For grown-ups

How it works

Four chapters follow the unit’s lessons in order. Each step shows one idea with something to change, then a quick check. A wrong answer names the likely mistake and gives one more try.

Two kinds of equations

IM separates px + q = r (equal parts plus one more) from p(x + q) = r (equal groups of two things). Tape diagrams show the difference, and hangers show why doing the same thing to both sides keeps an equation true.

Inequalities by testing

Rather than memorizing when to flip the sign, students solve the matching equation for the boundary, then test a number to see which side the solutions are on. Testing also shows why dividing by a negative turns the sign around.

Typing negative numbers

Quick checks with negative answers open the full keyboard, which has a minus sign. A hyphen works too.

Practice

Each chapter ends with a link to the matching station in Code Breakers, the practice game for this unit. Progress is saved on this device.
