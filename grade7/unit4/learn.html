Proportional Relationships & Percentages

Short lessons you can play with. Find rates from fractions, grow things by half again, raise and lower prices by a percent, add tax and tips, hunt for deals, and measure percent error, then check yourself. When you’re ready, practice in Mall Rush.

For grown-ups

How it works

Five chapters follow the unit’s lessons in order. Each step shows one idea with something to change, then a quick check. A wrong answer names the likely mistake and gives one more try.

Percents as multiplying

The unit’s thread is that a percent change is a proportional relationship: up 20% multiplies by 1.2, and “half as much again” multiplies by 3/2. Seeing it as one multiplication makes it easier to work backward to the original amount.

Of what?

Most percent mistakes come from taking the percent of the wrong amount. Every chapter asks what the percent is of: the old price for a percent change, the price before tax, the real value for percent error.

Practice

Each chapter ends with a link to the matching station in Mall Rush, the practice game for this unit. Progress is saved on this device.
