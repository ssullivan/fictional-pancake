For grown-ups

How a round works

Each station deals 8 fresh problems (Mall Madness deals 10). A right answer on the first try earns 10 points. After a wrong answer, the game names the likely mistake and gives one more try for 5 points.

The "Show me" hint

Hints use the tools from class: a double number line from 0% to 100% and past it, finding 1% or 10% first, and multiplying by 1 plus the fraction or percent.

Numbers you can do in your head

Prices are friendly ($40, $60, $120), percents are multiples of 5 (plus tax rates like 6% and fees like 0.5%), and fractions of an hour are halves, thirds, and quarters, so the challenge is the percent reasoning, not the arithmetic. Money answers are checked to the cent.

Mistakes it catches

Giving only the increase or the tax instead of the new total, taking a percent of the new price when it was of the old one, treating 20% as $20, multiplying by 0.2 instead of 1.2, dividing time by distance, and comparing an error to the measurement instead of the real size.

Not covered

Lesson 16 (Posing Percentage Problems) asks students to write their own problems, so it works better in class than as scored problems.
