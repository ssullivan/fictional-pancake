For grown-ups

How a round works

Each station deals 8 fresh problems (the Grand Prize deals 10). A right answer on the first try earns 10 points. After a wrong answer, the game names the likely mistake and gives one more try for 5 points. A question with only two choices (is the difference meaningful?) gets one try.

Typing probabilities

Probabilities can be typed as fractions (3/8) or decimals (0.375). The answer box opens a keyboard with a slash on phones.

The "Show me" hint

Hints mark the outcomes that count in the table of 36 sums for two number cubes, light up the path in a tree diagram, and draw the mean line on a dot plot.

Mistakes it catches

A count instead of a probability, comparing to the other outcomes instead of all of them, the probability of the opposite, adding the outcomes of two steps instead of multiplying, treating sums of two cubes as equally likely, samples that aren’t random, the sample instead of the whole population, and a gap in means not measured in MADs.

Not covered

Lesson 10 (designing simulations) and Lesson 20 (a class memory test) are hands-on experiments, so they work better in class than as scored problems.
