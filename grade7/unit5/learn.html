Rational Number Arithmetic

Short lessons you can play with. Add and subtract signed numbers with temperatures and jumps on a number line, multiply with speed and direction, divide and use negative rates, and solve equations with negative numbers, then check yourself. When you’re ready, practice in Deep Dive.

For grown-ups

How it works

Five chapters follow the unit’s lessons in order. Each step shows one idea with something to change, then a quick check. A wrong answer names the likely mistake and gives one more try.

Meaning before rules

The sign rules come from situations: temperatures rising and falling, jumps on a number line, and a boat’s position (velocity × time, with west and the past negative). Only then are the rules stated, so “a negative times a negative is positive” has a reason behind it.

Subtraction

Subtraction is shown two ways IM uses: as “what do you add?” (the jump from one number to the other) and as adding the opposite.

Typing negative numbers

Quick checks with negative answers open the full keyboard, which has a minus sign. A hyphen works too.

Practice

Each chapter ends with a link to the matching station in Deep Dive, the practice game for this unit. Progress is saved on this device.
