For grown-ups

How a round works

Each station deals 8 fresh problems (The Abyss deals 10). A right answer on the first try earns 10 points. After a wrong answer, the game names the likely mistake and gives one more try for 5 points. A question with only two choices (positive or negative?) gets one try.

Typing negative numbers

Answers can be negative, so the answer box opens the full keyboard, which has a minus sign (phone number pads don’t). Either a hyphen or a true minus sign works.

The "Show me" hint

Hints draw sums as jumps on a number line, mark temperatures on a thermometer and depths below sea level, and turn subtraction into adding the opposite.

Numbers you can do in your head

Numbers stay between −20 and 20 (depths and heights in friendly tens), and multiplying and dividing stay within the 12 × 12 facts, so the challenge is the signs, not the arithmetic.

Mistakes it catches

The wrong sign on a sum, adding the distances from zero when the signs differ, subtracting a negative as if it were positive, a difference taken the wrong way round, a negative times a negative called negative, a past position on the wrong side, and undoing multiplication by subtracting.

Not covered

Lesson 17 (The Stock Market) is a research project, so it works better in class than as scored problems.
