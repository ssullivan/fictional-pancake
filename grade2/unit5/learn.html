Numbers to 1,000

Short lessons you can play with. Make hundreds from tens, build and name three-digit numbers, write them in expanded form, and find, compare, and order them, then check yourself. Tap Read to me to hear any step read aloud.

For grown-ups

How it works

Five short chapters follow the unit’s lessons in order. Each step shows one idea with something to tap or move, then a quick check. A wrong answer names the likely mistake and gives one more try.

Read to me

The Read to me buttons read each step and quick check aloud with the device’s built-in voice, for students still learning to read. Progress is saved on this device.

Numbers

All numbers are within 1,000. The blocks are base-ten diagrams: a big square is a hundred, a stick is a ten, and a small square is a one. There’s no adding or subtracting in this unit; that comes in Unit 7.

The big idea

10 ones make a ten and 10 tens make a hundred, so each digit’s place tells what it’s worth. The same number can be built different ways, like 3 hundreds and 14 tens for 440.

Not covered

Center Days (Lessons 7 and 13) and Hundreds of Objects (Lesson 14) are optional lessons that work better in class. Practice it all in Dragon Duel, the game for this unit.
