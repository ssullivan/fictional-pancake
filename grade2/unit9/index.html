Field Trip

The whole class is going on a field trip! Practice facts on the bus, measure paths on the trail map, explore the number museum, sell tickets, and act out stories. Then ride the bus home. Each station matches lessons from class. Tap Read to me to hear any problem.

For grown-ups

How a round works

Each station deals 8 fresh problems (Ride Home deals 10). A right answer on the first try earns 10 points. After a wrong answer, the game names the likely mistake and gives one more try for 5 points. In Ride Home, each right answer takes the bus one of 10 stops closer to school.

Read to me

The Read to me button reads the problem, the choices, and the feedback aloud with the device’s built-in voice. It never reads the picture, since that could give the answer away.

Numbers

Facts stay within 20, map paths are whole centimeters, and numbers are within 1,000. Problems that ask for a number need at most one new or broken ten or hundred, or a jump to a hundred and back (like 398 + 245).

Mistakes it catches

Subtracting when a fact adds, leaving out a part of a path, adding paths when asked how much shorter, writing 14 tens as 14 in the tens place, trading a hundred for 10 ones, adding instead of taking away the extra when jumping to a hundred, taking the smaller digit from the bigger one, and mixing up “in all” and “how many more.”

Learn it first

Each station matches a chapter of the Learn page for this unit, which walks through the ideas step by step.

Not covered

Lesson 2 (Fluency Flip) is a card game best played with a partner, and writing your own story problems (Lessons 12–13) works better in class.
