Sweet Shop

Welcome to the Sweet Shop! Share treats fairly, sort odd and even, line up cookies in arrays, and cut brownie pans into squares. Then fill the bake sale tray. Each station matches lessons from class. Tap Read to me to hear any problem.

For grown-ups

How a round works

Each station deals 8 fresh problems (the Bake Sale deals 10). A right answer on the first try earns 10 points. After a wrong answer, the game names the likely mistake and gives one more try for 5 points. At the Bake Sale, each right answer puts one of 10 cupcakes on the tray.

Read to me

The Read to me button reads the problem, the choices, and the feedback aloud with the device’s built-in voice. It never reads the picture, since that could give the answer away.

Numbers

Odd and even numbers are within 20. Arrays have up to 5 rows and 5 columns, so every total is 25 or less and can be found by adding equal addends or counting on.

Mistakes it catches

Giving all of a share to one friend, thinking 2 left over means no partner, calling an odd number even, mixing up rows and columns, adding the number of rows to the number in a row, counting only one row or the squares around the edge, and calling rectangles or squares of different sizes “same-size squares.”

Learn it first

Each station matches a chapter of the Learn page for this unit, which walks through the ideas step by step.

Not covered

Patterns with Even and Odd Numbers (Lesson 5) and the Center Days (Lessons 6 and 13) are optional lessons that work better in class.
