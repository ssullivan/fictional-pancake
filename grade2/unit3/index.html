Inchworm Trail

Follow the trail with a little inchworm! Measure with cubes and rulers, meet the reptiles, tie silk, and read line plots. Then help the inchworm crawl to the leaf. Each station matches lessons from class. Tap Read to me to hear any problem.

For grown-ups

How a round works

Each station deals 8 fresh problems (the Big Crawl deals 10). A right answer on the first try earns 10 points. After a wrong answer, the game names the likely mistake and gives one more try for 5 points. In the Big Crawl, each right answer moves the inchworm 1 inch closer to the leaf.

Read to me

The Read to me button reads the problem, the choices, and the feedback aloud with the device’s built-in voice. It never reads the picture, since that could give the answer away.

Numbers

Lengths are whole centimeters, inches, feet, and meters within 100, and things have real-life sizes: a crayon is about 9 cm, a leopard gecko about 20 cm, a bench 4 or 5 feet. Length stories need at most one new or broken ten.

Mistakes it catches

Counting the marks on a ruler instead of the spaces, reading where a thing ends when it doesn’t start at 0 (or on a torn tape), thinking a foot is 10 inches, using the same number of centimeters as inches, adding when a length is cut off or compared, and reading the length instead of counting the Xs on a line plot.

Learn it first

Each station matches a chapter of the Learn page for this unit, which walks through the ideas step by step.

Not covered

Center Days (Lessons 7, 13, and 17) and Make a Yard Stick (Lesson 18) are hands-on, optional lessons that work better in class. Measuring real things with a real ruler is best done at home or in class, too.
