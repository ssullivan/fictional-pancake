Robot Factory

Welcome to the robot factory! Count on the conveyor belt, add and take away parts by place, and trade up tens and hundreds. Then power up the robot. Each station matches lessons from class. Tap Read to me to hear any problem.

For grown-ups

How a round works

Each station deals 8 fresh problems (Robot Rollout deals 10). A right answer on the first try earns 10 points. After a wrong answer, the game names the likely mistake and gives one more try for 5 points. In Robot Rollout, each right answer lights one of the 10 lights on the robot’s chest.

Read to me

The Read to me button reads the problem, the choices, and the feedback aloud with the device’s built-in voice. It never reads the picture, since that could give the answer away.

Numbers

All numbers are within 1,000, shown with base-ten blocks on a place-value mat (a big square is a hundred, a stick is a ten, a small square is a one) and open number lines. Problems that ask for a number need at most one new or broken ten or hundred; adding a number like 199 is done by jumping 200 and back 1. Problems with two trades ask what needs to happen instead.

Mistakes it catches

Skipping a jump, changing the wrong digit for 10 or 100 more or less, adding when asked for a difference, taking 4 for 40, forgetting a new ten or hundred (or writing 13 ones as 13 in the ones place), flipping the digits to take the smaller from the bigger instead of breaking a ten or hundred, and forgetting that a broken ten or hundred leaves one fewer.

Learn it first

Each station matches a chapter of the Learn page for this unit, which walks through the ideas step by step.

Not covered

Center Days (Lessons 5, 11, and 17) and Paint Splattered Bar Graph (Lesson 18) are optional lessons that work better in class.
