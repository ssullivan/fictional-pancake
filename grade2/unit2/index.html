Market Day

It’s market day at the farm! Sell apples, pick berries, break carrot bunches, and fill flower buckets. Then load the pumpkin wagon. Each station matches lessons from class. Tap Read to me to hear any problem.

For grown-ups

How a round works

Each station deals 8 fresh problems (the Pumpkin Wagon deals 10). A right answer on the first try earns 10 points. After a wrong answer, the game names the likely mistake and gives one more try for 5 points. At the Pumpkin Wagon, each right answer loads one of 10 pumpkins.

Read to me

The Read to me button reads the problem, the choices, and the feedback aloud with the device’s built-in voice. It never reads the picture, since that could give the answer away.

Numbers

Everything stays within 100, with base-ten blocks for tens and ones. Each problem needs at most one new ten or one broken ten, so the idea is the challenge, not the arithmetic.

Mistakes it catches

Adding when a story asks how many more, taking the smaller ones digit from the bigger one instead of breaking a ten, breaking a ten without taking it from the tens, forgetting the new ten (or writing 7 tens and 13 ones as 713), jumping back 1 for a ten, counting jumps instead of adding them, and mixing up the whole and the parts in a diagram.

Learn it first

Each station matches a chapter of the Learn page for this unit, which walks through the ideas step by step.

Not covered

Center Days (Lessons 4, 10, and 15) and Our Market’s Inventory (Lesson 16) are hands-on, optional lessons that work better in class.
